I am a Graduate Student in Data Science at the [University of Houston-Clear Lake](https://www.uhcl.edu/) (CGPA: 3.89/4), where I also serve as a Graduate Teaching Assistant in the Computing Science Department. I hold a B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning from CVR College of Engineering. My work spans machine learning, open-source AI tooling, and applied software development, with a focus on building practical systems that bridge research and real-world use.

My interests include machine learning, natural language processing, intelligent agent systems, and computational biology. I am particularly drawn to projects where data-driven methods solve meaningful problems — whether in healthcare, education, or developer tooling.

In addition to my studies, I am committed to knowledge sharing through mentoring, teaching, and my [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ThuraabTech) where I teach machine learning and data science concepts to a broader audience.
